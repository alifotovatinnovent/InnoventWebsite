// Architecture

The five-layer Innfini stack.

Every deployment runs on a unified, edge-to-cloud architecture. Each layer is independently scalable, observable, and replaceable — but composes into a single agentic runtime.

  1. L5
    Agentic AI orchestration. Goal-driven agents reason over live sensor state, plan multi-step actions across systems, and document every decision in an audit trail. Policy guardrails enforce safety, scope and approval thresholds.
  2. L4
    Decision engine & ML models. Vision (detection, OCR, anomaly), time-series forecasters, optimization solvers, and LLM-grounded reasoning — all served through one model gateway with versioning and shadow-deploy.
  3. L3
    Unified object & event graph. Every asset, place, person, work order, and signal is a typed node. Events stream through a Kafka-grade backbone with schema enforcement and idempotent processing.
  4. L2
    Connectors & integrations. 80+ pre-built connectors for ERP (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite), WMS (Manhattan, Blue Yonder), MES, SCADA, CMMS, GIS, and identity — plus a typed SDK.
  5. L1
    Sensor fabric (edge). RFID gateways (Zebra, Nordic ID, Impinj), computer-vision cameras, BLE beacons, LoRaWAN, environmental and biometric sensors. Sub-100ms inference at the edge.

Why this architecture matters.

Most "AI platforms" are a model gateway bolted onto a data warehouse. That works for analytics. It does not work for operations, where a decision has to traverse five systems, three policies, and a physical actuator in under a second.

Innfini treats the stack as one runtime. Agents reason on live graph state — not stale snapshots — and act through the same integration layer they read from. Every action is reversible, every decision is auditable.
