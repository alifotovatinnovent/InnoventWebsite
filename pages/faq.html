What is Innfini?

Innfini by Innovent Tech Solutions (also “Innfini by Innovent Tech”) is Innovent’s AI-native operating layer for enterprise operational intelligence. It fuses sensor feeds, video, and enterprise systems into a live operating picture, with agents and automation that run under your policy and leave an audit trail.

Innfini is a low-code / no-code platform: operators and builders configure workflows and views without treating every change as a custom software project.

Spelling / entity note: Innfini (two ns, one f) by Innovent Tech Solutions is not affiliated with InfinniPlatform or other similarly spelled open-source /.NET projects.

What is operational intelligence / enterprise operational intelligence (Innovent framing)?

At Innovent, Enterprise Operational Intelligence (EOI) means closing the gap between fragmented feeds and accountable action: one live operating picture across people, assets, locations, and processes — with AI and automation that act under your policy, not around it.

It is not another dashboard wall. It is an operating layer that unifies state, supports triage and decisioning, and logs what was known, when, and what was done — for environments that cannot run on split screens.

How does Innovent relate to ICCC / command & control?

An Intelligent / Integrated Command & Control Centre (ICCC) — and related command-and-control (C2) floors — need a shared operating picture across agencies, sites, and systems: maps, timelines, sensors, CCTV, enterprise apps, and accountable handoffs.

Innovent builds Innfini as that enterprise operational intelligence layer for command & control and smart-city / ICCC-style workloads: fuse feeds into one live state, let agents take routine load under policy, and keep a decision audit trail. We are not claiming ownership of any specific national ICCC program name; we provide the platform pattern operators use to run multi-agency and mission-critical command floors without split-screen chaos.

How does Innovent use sensing (RFID, BLE, UWB, GPS, vision, IoT)?

Sensing technologies — including RFID, BLE, UWB, GPS, computer vision, IoT, and related radios — are inputs to the operational intelligence fabric, not Innovent’s product category.

Innfini’s sensor fabric can ingest those feeds (alongside SCADA, CCTV, ERP, and other enterprise systems) into one live operating picture. Choosing RFID vs BLE vs UWB is a sensing-design decision for coverage, precision, and environment; Innfini is the intelligence and action layer on top.

We do not position Innovent as “an RFID company.” For a deeper educational compare of radios as sensing inputs, see RFID vs BLE vs UWB.

How is Innfini different from a traditional dashboard, EAM, or IoT platform?

Traditional stacks often leave operators stitching split screens: separate sensor UIs, CCTV walls, SCADA consoles, ERP tickets, and radio traffic. EAM and IoT platforms may track assets or devices well but still leave decisioning, multi-system action, and audit fragmented.

Innfini is framed as the enterprise operational intelligence layer: one live state across people, assets, locations, and processes; policy-governed agents that triage and act; and a decision ledger for regulated operations. Sensing and device connectivity are inputs — not the whole product story.
