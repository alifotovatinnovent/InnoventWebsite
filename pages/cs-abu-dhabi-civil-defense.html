// Challenges

The brief

  • 01
    Manual equipment checks. Confirming what was loaded on each appliance depended on crews working through paper checklists under time pressure, at the worst possible moment.
  • 02
    Equipment lost on the ground. Items left at an incident scene were often only discovered days later, during the next inventory count rather than on return.
  • 03
    Readiness could not be evidenced. Station and appliance readiness was asserted rather than demonstrated, with no per-item record behind it.
  • 04
    Two systems of record, no bridge. Asset data in the ERP and operational state in the readiness platform were reconciled by hand, so neither was fully trusted.
// Solution

Innfini as the equipment readiness layer.

Innovent tagged every piece of firefighting equipment across 10 stations with RFID and made the appliance itself the reader. Loading an appliance now produces a record rather than a signature: the system knows what is aboard, what is missing and what came back, and writes that state into the agency's ERP and readiness platform without anyone retyping it.

RFID asset tagging

Every firefighting equipment item carries a rugged RFID tag, registered once against its ERP asset record.

On-truck verification

The appliance reads its own load, so what is aboard and what is missing is known without a manual count.

Pre-mission check

Loading produces an automatic readiness record before the appliance leaves the station.

Post-mission check

On return the same read flags anything not recovered, while the scene is still reachable.

ERP integration

Asset state, stock levels and maintenance status flow straight into the agency's ERP with no re-entry.

Readiness platform

Station and appliance readiness is derived from real per-item data rather than declared.

A crew leaves the station knowing exactly what is on the truck, and returns knowing exactly what came back — without a clipboard in sight.
// Business impact

Readiness you can evidence.

  • 01
    Per-item readiness across 10 stations. Every appliance's load state is known and evidenced, not asserted.
  • 02
    Faster turnout. Pre-departure verification happens automatically as the appliance is loaded, instead of holding the crew at the door.
  • 03
    Nothing left behind. The post-mission check flags a missing item on return, while the scene can still be revisited.
  • 04
    One reconciled asset record. ERP and readiness platform stay in step automatically, so both can be trusted for audit and procurement.
