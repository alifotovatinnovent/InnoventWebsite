// Challenges

The brief

  • 01
    Fragmented plant operations. Manual systems across raw material handling, production, logistics, facility management and quality control limited plant-wide visibility.
  • 02
    No supply-chain view. No real-time view of inbound supply, raw material inventory, work-in-progress, batch status or finished-goods movement.
  • 03
    Paper-based quality control. Manual visual inspection of intermediates and finished products drove inconsistent results, slower batch release and avoidable rework.
  • 04
    Unified automation needed. A single platform automating logistics, process operations, IoT, facility management and AI-driven quality control across the plant.
// Solution

Innfini as the operating layer.

Innovent deployed Innfini as the integrated digital-operations platform for Ecovyst's US chemical plant — automating the entire manufacturing chain end-to-end, from raw-material intake through process operations to AI-driven quality release, under one platform.

Manufacturing logistics

Inbound/outbound logistics, dispatch, yard management and finished-goods movement orchestrated end-to-end.

Raw material management

Material receipt, weigh-in, batch traceability, inventory and consumption tracking integrated with the production lines.

Process automation

Real-time orchestration of plant operations, recipe execution, batch tracking and exception handling across every production line.

IoT deployment

Sensor-agnostic ingestion of temperature, pressure, flow, level, vibration and tank telemetry via MQTT.

Facility management

Integrated BMS, access control, CCTV, energy and utilities monitoring across the plant footprint.

Vision AI quality

Image-processing pipelines for automated visual inspection, defect detection and quality classification on the line.

A US specialty-chemical plant, fully automated from raw-material intake through process operations to AI-driven visual quality release — under one platform.
// Business impact

The outcome.

  • 01
    Fully automated chain. From raw-material receipt through process operations to finished-goods dispatch — every plant function under one platform.
  • 02
    Real-time plant-wide visibility. Raw materials, work-in-progress, finished goods, IoT telemetry and facility systems all visible live.
  • 03
    Consistent quality release. AI-powered visual inspection reduces manual inspection effort and rework — faster batch release.
  • 04
    Process safety & compliance. Improved safety, energy efficiency and compliance through unified 24/7 IoT and facility monitoring.
