// How the Sensor Fabric works

From physical signal to digital intelligence.

Five layers. INNFINI captures from any device, processes at the edge where it matters, normalizes every signal into the same event format — and feeds it into the agents, dashboards and workflows that act on it.

  1. 01 · Devices

    Connect Any Sensor

    RFID, BLE, GPS, vision, barcode, environmental, mobile, PLC, BMS — any device, any brand, any protocol.

  2. 02 · Edge

    Process Locally

    Filter, dedupe, buffer and apply local rules at the edge. Stay online and operational even when the network drops.

  3. 03 · Normalize

    Common Format

    Raw reads, scans, pixels and readings are converted into one shared operational event schema across every signal type.

  4. 04 · Events

    Business Events

    Each signal becomes a meaningful event — asset entered Zone A · temp exceeded threshold · vehicle crossed gate-3.

  5. 05 · Intelligence

    Feed AI & Dashboards

    Clean, structured events stream into agents, ML models, dashboards, workflows and enterprise integrations.

// Layer 02 · Environmental & climate sensing

The most common cause of value loss in transit is not theft.

It is condition. Environmental channels turn a delivery record into a quality record — with the achievable accuracy stated per channel, not one headline number.

Humidity — the honest caveat

Liquid water on a capacitive RH sensor makes it non-responsive until it dries. We flag dew-point crossings rather than reporting a number we cannot stand behind.

Light — false-alarm suppression

Absolute lux thresholds fire constantly in a lit warehouse. We use relative-change detection on a 10-second sample with an events-per-hour cap, so a tamper alert means something.

// Layer 02 · Shock, tilt & handling integrity

Damage claims are won with waveform data, not a broken sticker.

We deploy the recording tier the cargo value justifies — from a two-dollar indicator to a 4,096 sps evidence-grade recorder.

Tier 1

Passive indicator

  • Single-use mechanical impact and tilt indicators
  • Thresholds 5, 10, 15, 25, 37, 50, 75 G
  • Tilt at 30°–90° with direction, or >80° overturn
  • No power, no configuration, ±15 % accuracy
  • Visual deterrent at the lowest possible cost
Tier 2

Connected tracker

  • Shock events on TRK-450 / TAG-Micro, ~12 G sensitivity
  • Tilt and orientation logged with position
  • Motion, dwell and free-fall detection
  • Alert raised in transit, not at delivery
  • Correlated with GPS trace and custody events
Tier 3

Impact recorder

  • SHOCK-R2: triaxial, 1–200 g full-scale selectable
  • 4,096 samples/s per axis — captures pulse shape
  • Tilt and roll ±180° at 0.1° resolution
  • Pre-trigger buffer; 800+ detailed events stored
  • Evidence grade for ASTM D3332 / ISTA disputes

// Layer 02 · Tamper, access & intrusion sensing

Six independent channels. Defeating all six silently is not possible.

Any one of them can raise a breach. Defeating one is possible; defeating all six, in transit, without a trace, is not.

Light ingress0.01 lux detection with relative-change logic→ Box or container opened
Door & reed contact20–30 mm magnetic detection, open-duration timing→ Door cycled outside an authorised window
Cable-cut / bolt shearContinuity loop on the SEAL-S2 seal body→ Seal defeated — instant alarm
Dis-bond / removalAdhesion and proximity sensing on TAG-Micro→ Tracker peeled off the asset
Motion while dormantAccelerometer armed when the shipment should be at rest→ Asset moved in storage without authority
NFC tamper loopLoop break permanently written into the tag record→ Single-use, phone-verifiable at any checkpoint
Access control

Every LOCK-A1 carries a per-shipment authorised-user list. An unlock is matched against the list, the geofence and the schedule before it is granted — and every attempt, granted or refused, is written to the custody ledger with the card ID.
