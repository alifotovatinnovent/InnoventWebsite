// Operating challenges

The brief

  • 01
    TOS / WMS / DC silos. Terminal operating systems, WMS, fleet, access control, supplier portals — each in a silo. Visibility ends where one system stops.
  • 02
    Cold-chain risk. Temperature-sensitive freight at risk between handoffs. Spot checks miss breaches; insurance claims keep climbing.
  • 03
    Yard labor and dwell. Hostlers, gate clerks and dock supervisors coordinate by radio and clipboard. Labor is high; throughput is bottlenecked; errors compound.
  • 04
    Last-mile orchestration. Onward delivery coordinated through email and phone. ETAs are approximations; customers can't plan.
