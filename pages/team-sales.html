// The challenge

The brief

  • 01
    Long-cycle deals. Public-sector and enterprise deals run 6–18 months. The AE who isn't patient doesn't close.
  • 02
    Multi-stakeholder complexity. A C&C deal touches operations, IT, security, procurement, legal and executive sponsors. The AE has to navigate all of them.
  • 03
    Technical credibility. Customers in defense, public safety and critical infrastructure don't buy from non-technical reps. Technical fluency is the bar.
  • 04
    Evidence-based selling. No fluff, no FUD. The customer sees real deployments, real outcomes, real numbers. The AE who oversells doesn't make it past pilot.
