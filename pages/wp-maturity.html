The Operations AI Maturity Model
Q3 2024 · 24 pages

Operations leaders ask us a version of the same question: "are we ready for agentic AI?" The honest answer is "depends on where you are." This paper proposes a maturity model — five stages — to make that question precise.

The five stages

Stage 1 — Dashboarded. Data is visible. Decisions are manual. The organization can see operations but can't intervene faster than the next standup.

Stage 2 — Alerted. Anomaly detection fires. Operators get pinged. The pace of intervention shortens from days to hours.

Stage 3 — Recommended. Models propose actions. Operators approve. Decision time compresses to minutes. Cognitive load increases.

Stage 4 — Supervised agentic. Agents act under policy with operator approval at thresholds. Routine decisions automate. Operator span of control grows.

Stage 5 — Autonomous agentic. Agents act independently within policy. Operators handle exceptions. The operating model is fundamentally different.

Self-assessment

The paper includes a 30-item self-assessment with scoring guidance. Most operations organizations sit between stages 2 and 3. The interesting question is what it takes to move.
