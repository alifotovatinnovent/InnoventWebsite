// Challenges

The brief

  • 01
    Complex multi-site terminal ops. Port and terminal operations across multiple sites with fragmented systems — limiting end-to-end visibility into warehousing, logistics and terminal flows.
  • 02
    No real-time view. Asset location, vehicle movement, container status, supplier deliveries and environmental conditions sat in separate systems.
  • 03
    Cold-chain integrity at risk. Manual coordination with suppliers and contractors slowed scheduling, dispatch and last-mile delivery — and put cold-chain integrity at risk.
  • 04
    Unification mandate. A single digital platform unifying every operational system, sensor, and supplier/contractor interface across the port — the explicit AD Ports brief.
// Solution

Innfini as the operating layer.

Innovent deployed Innfini as the enterprise digital-operations platform for AD Ports Group — delivering complete visibility across warehousing, logistics and terminal operations and unifying every system under one platform.

Warehousing & logistics

Full visibility across all storage locations, inventory movement, picking, dispatch and stock control.

Cold-chain management

Continuous temperature and condition monitoring via deployed temperature loggers and IoT sensors.

Access control, BMS & CCTV

Integrated physical security and building management for site-wide situational awareness.

Supplier & contractor portals

Self-service scheduling, delivery booking, dispatch coordination and document exchange.

Last-mile & fleet

Vehicle telemetry, GPS tracking and delivery orchestration across operational zones.

Dashboards & CEP rules

Configurable map-based reporting, MQTT integration and complex-event-processing rules driving automation across terminal operations.

Port-wide visibility, end-to-end logistics and 24/7 cold-chain integrity — one operating layer across every site, every supplier, every container.
// Business impact

The outcome.

  • 01
    Complete operational visibility. Warehousing, logistics, terminals and the entire port footprint surfaced through one operating picture.
  • 02
    End-to-end cold-chain integrity. Real-time temperature monitoring and automated alerts across the network.
  • 03
    Streamlined supplier coordination. Unified scheduling, delivery and document portals for every contractor and supplier.
  • 04
    Optimized fleet & last-mile. GPS, telemetry and workflow automation reduce manual reporting and predictive alerts cut reactive maintenance events.
