// Who it's for

City operators who need one executable picture.

Cities still run on split screens: traffic in one tool, CCTV on a wall, CAD in another console, 311 in a queue, utility SCADA elsewhere, and citizen reports arriving by radio or email. Humans stitch the city together under pressure. When something escalates across agencies, the audit trail is incomplete — and leadership, partners, and the public all ask: what did we know, when, and what did we do?

Enterprise Operational Intelligence means closing that gap: one live operating picture across people, assets, locations, and processes — with AI and automation that act under your policy, not around it.

// Solution

One ICCC canvas. Agents under policy. Accountable civic decisions.

One operating picture across every department, sensor, and system.

Fuse CCTV, traffic and transit feeds, CAD / emergency signals, IoT, utility telemetry, GIS, citizen reports, and enterprise systems — including RFID as one sensor input among many — into a single live civic state. Toggle layers, geofence districts, drill in, and take action without leaving the ICCC console.

People, assets, locations, and processes appear on one graph — so the command floor sees relationships across agencies, not silos.

AI agents that triage, decide, and act under policy.

Agents handle the routine civic load — propose diversions, notify field teams, escalate multi-agency incidents, open service workflows, and surface next-best actions — always inside policy guardrails and approval thresholds. Operators keep authority where it matters.

Automation and AI reduce operator cognitive load without removing human accountability on the ICCC floor.

Every decision logged. Every model accountable.

Built for regulated and sovereign civic environments — decision audit trails, role-based and agency-scoped controls, residency options, and deployment models that match your security posture.

// Capabilities

Enterprise Operational Intelligence for smart-city / ICCC.

  • Unified civic operating picture — Live state of people, assets, places, incidents, work orders, and city events on one object & event graph.
  • Sensor-agnostic inputs — Edge sensor fabric for CCTV / computer vision, IoT, BLE, LoRaWAN, environmental sensors, traffic detection, SCADA / utility telemetry, and more.
  • Enterprise & civic connectors — Pre-built connectors across ERP, GIS, CAD/RMS bridges, identity, data/BI, and city systems.
  • Decision engine & ML — Vision, time-series forecasting, optimization, and grounded reasoning through one model gateway.
  • Agentic orchestration — Goal-driven agents that plan multi-step civic actions across systems and document every decision in an audit trail, with policy guardrails.
  • Governed automation — RBAC/ABAC, agency-scoped views, approval thresholds, reversible actions, and a decision ledger for accountable city operations.
  • Flexible deployment — Cloud, private cloud, on-premises (including air-gapped / restricted), and hybrid edge.
  • Operator interfaces — ICCC / control tower, layer-fusion maps, heatmaps/geofences, and conversational ops grounded in live civic state.
