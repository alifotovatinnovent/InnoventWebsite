The problem: cities run on split screens

Traffic sits in one tool, CCTV on a wall, CAD in another console, service requests in a queue, utility SCADA elsewhere, and citizen reports arrive by radio or email. Operators stitch the city together under pressure. When something escalates across agencies, the record is incomplete, and leadership, partners and the public ask: what did we know, when, and what did we do?

One live civic operating picture

Innfini brings together city feeds and enterprise systems into one live civic state. People, assets, locations and processes appear on one graph, so the ICCC floor sees relationships across agencies, not silos. Operators toggle layers, geofence districts, drill in and act without leaving the console.

How an event becomes owned work

  1. Sense. Feeds from cameras, sensors, city systems and citizen reports arrive in one live civic state.
  2. Triage. AI-assisted triage groups related events and surfaces next-best actions for the operator.
  3. Assign. Work is routed to a named owner and agency, with a deadline and approval thresholds under policy.
  4. Act. Operators approve or adjust; automation notifies field teams, opens service workflows or escalates across agencies.
  5. Review. Every decision is logged, so after-action review can answer what was known, when, and what was done.

Frequently asked questions

What is a smart-city ICCC platform?

An integrated command and control centre (ICCC) platform gives city operators one live operating picture across departments, sensors and systems, with shared maps, a shared timeline and accountable handoffs between agencies. Innfini by Innovent Tech Solutions provides that layer as Enterprise Operational Intelligence via Innfini.

Which feeds and systems can Innfini bring into an ICCC?

Innfini brings together CCTV and computer vision, traffic and transit feeds, CAD and emergency signals, IoT and environmental sensors, utility telemetry and SCADA, GIS, citizen reports and enterprise systems such as ERP, identity and data/BI. RFID is one sensor input among many, not the product category.

How does AI work on the ICCC floor?

AI-assisted workflows handle routine civic load under policy: proposing diversions, notifying field teams, escalating multi-agency incidents and opening service workflows. Approval thresholds keep operators in authority where it matters, and every action is recorded in a decision audit trail.

Is the ICCC platform different from a BI dashboard?

Yes. BI dashboards report on historical data. An ICCC running on Innfini works on live events and turns them into owned work with a named owner, a deadline and an audit trail, and it connects to existing data/BI systems as inputs rather than replacing them.
