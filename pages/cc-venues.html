// The challenge

The brief

  • 01
    Crowd-flow blind spots. Concourse density and chokepoints emerge in seconds. Manual observation can't keep up; cameras alone don't aggregate.
  • 02
    Weapon-detection coordination. Modern weapon-detection systems generate alerts faster than security teams can dispatch. Triage is the bottleneck.
  • 03
    Medical response. Cardiac and other emergencies among large crowds need sub-90-second dispatch. Locating the patient is the first 30 seconds.
  • 04
    Multi-feed video sprawl. A typical major venue runs 200–400 camera feeds. Operators can watch four. The rest is forensic, not preventive.
// How Innfini delivers

What we ship.

Six operating surfaces, one venue command layer. Each is a real Innfini screen from an illustrative matchday.

01 · Crowd flow

Crowd flow AI

See the build-up before it becomes a crush.

Computer vision across gates, concourses and plazas turns every camera into a density sensor. Innfini aggregates them into one pedestrian-density map, watches the arrival trend against the event clock, and predicts where a chokepoint will form minutes before it does.

  • Live density heatmap · gates, concourses, plazas, transit links
  • Predictive bottleneck alerts · 5–10 minutes ahead
  • Guided diversion · alternate gates and live wayfinding to visitors
  • Arrival trend against event start · main entrances vs alternative routes
02 · Security triage

Weapon-detection integration

Alerts arrive faster than teams can dispatch. Triage is the product.

Innfini fuses natively with weapon-detection lanes so every sensor alert lands as a case, not a beep: the lane, the camera frame, the timestamp and the nearest supervisor, together. An agent scores and routes it; the operator reviews with the video already open; the team is assigned with one action.

  • Native integration with weapon-detection vendors · lane by lane
  • Alert → operator review → team assigned · timed and logged
  • Camera frame and context attached automatically
  • Supervisor routing to the lane · response metrics live
03 · Medical response

Medical response ops

Locating the patient is the first 30 seconds. Innfini removes them.

A fan collapses in Section 314. The venue app or a steward pins the seat; Innfini finds the nearest medical team and the nearest RTLS-tagged AED, maps the route through the concourse and the right stair, and tracks the team until they confirm arrival — with the whole sequence on the clock.

  • Patient locator · venue-app opt-in or steward report, to the seat
  • RTLS-tagged AEDs and medical kits · nearest, always
  • Route mapped level by level · stair and access point named
  • Locate → Dispatch → Coordinate · sub-90-second target
04 · Video intelligence

Multi-feed video wall

Hundreds of feeds. Operators watch what matters.

A major venue runs 200–400 cameras and an operator can watch four. Innfini tags every feed with AI — crowd build-up, gate delay, turnstile queue — surfaces the flagged one, and auto-clips the moment onto the event timeline so review happens in seconds instead of hours afterwards.

  • AI-tagged feeds · crowd build-up, queues, obstructions
  • Flagged camera brought forward · others index themselves
  • Auto-clipping on triggers · clip attached to the event
  • Timeline scrub across the whole session · jump to any flag
05 · Asset accountability

Asset RFID

Every radio, kit and barrier — stored, issued, located, returned.

Comms gear, medical kits and crowd-management barriers are RFID-tagged and read at the store, the loading dock and the gates. Innfini keeps the count live through the event and closes the loop on return, so nothing is missing when the next event starts.

  • Radios, medical kits, barriers · counted live against inventory
  • Issue and return by identity · who has what, right now
  • RFID coverage at store, dock and gate
  • Store → Issue → Locate → Return · one accountable cycle
06 · Event review

Audit & after-action

The after-action writes itself.

Every alert, operator decision, dispatch and closure is captured with its time, its camera clip and the person who acted. When the event closes, Innfini replays the timeline on the map and produces the after-action report — ready for the debrief, the regulator and the next matchday plan.

  • Full timeline · alert, review, dispatch, closure — with clips
  • Map replay of the response route and every touchpoint
  • Operator and agent actions both on the record
  • After-action report generated · event, duration, status
When the cardiac arrest happens in section 314, the locator pings within 30 seconds. The nearest AED is on the way in 90. The video clip is auto-generated. The after-action writes itself. That is operational excellence.
