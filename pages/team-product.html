// The challenge

The brief

  • 01
    The users aren't consumers. A 911 dispatcher under load, a plant operator at 3am, a Civil Defense commander mid-event. The user research has to honor the context.
  • 02
    The stakes are real. A confusing UX in a consumer app is annoying. In a command center, it's a missed call.
  • 03
    Customer development in regulated environments. You can't A/B test on emergency response. Customer development has to be qualitative and operator-deep.
  • 04
    The stack is deep. Product decisions touch agentic runtime, edge inference, sensor fusion, integration layer. The PM bench needs depth.
// How Innfini delivers

What we ship.

Operator-deep customer development
