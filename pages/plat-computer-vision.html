// Why it matters

The problem

  • 01
    Industrial light is hard. Most vision models trained on consumer datasets fail on the shop floor — low light, glare, occlusion, motion.
  • 02
    Sites vary. A model that works at one site fails at the next. Lighting, angles, and object classes are too varied for generic models.
  • 03
    Drift is silent. Model accuracy degrades as operations evolve. Without continuous evaluation, you find out at customer cost.
  • 04
    RFID alone is positionless. RFID counts perfectly but tells you nothing about spatial placement beneath the antenna footprint.
