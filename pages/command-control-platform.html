One operating picture. Agents under policy. Every decision logged.

People, assets, locations, and processes appear on one graph — so the command floor sees relationships, not silos.

AI agents that triage, decide, and act under policy.

Agents handle the routine load — auto-dispatch maintenance, lock zones, notify responders, escalate only what needs a human in the loop — always inside policy guardrails and approval thresholds.

// Capabilities

Enterprise Operational Intelligence on Innfini.

  • Unified operating picture — Live state of people, assets, places, work orders, and events on one object & event graph.
  • Sensor-agnostic inputs — Edge sensor fabric for RFID, computer vision, BLE, LoRaWAN, environmental sensors, SCADA / Modbus / OPC-UA, and more — RFID is one input, not the product story.
  • Enterprise connectors — Pre-built connectors across ERP, WMS/TMS, MES/SCADA, identity, and data/BI systems.
  • Decision engine & ML — Vision, time-series forecasting, optimization, and grounded reasoning through one model gateway.
  • Agentic orchestration — Goal-driven agents that plan multi-step actions across systems and document every decision in an audit trail, with policy guardrails.
  • Governed automation — RBAC/ABAC, approval thresholds, reversible actions, and a decision ledger for regulated operations.
  • Flexible deployment — Cloud, private cloud, on-premises (including air-gapped / restricted), and hybrid edge.
  • Operator interfaces — Control tower, location maps, heatmaps/geofences, and conversational ops grounded in live state.
