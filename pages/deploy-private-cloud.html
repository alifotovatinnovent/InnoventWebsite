// Why it matters

The problem

  • 01
    Multi-tenancy concerns. Some customers have regulatory or policy requirements precluding multi-tenant SaaS.
  • 02
    Data sovereignty. Cross-border data movement runs into GDPR, local data-protection laws, and customer policy constraints.
  • 03
    Key management. Encryption keys held by the vendor concentrate risk. Customer-managed keys are increasingly required.
  • 04
    Audit access. Customer security teams need infrastructure-level audit access that multi-tenant SaaS can't provide.
// What Innfini delivers

The capability surface.

Single-tenant in your VPC

Innfini deploys in your AWS, Azure or GCP account. Your network, your IAM, your audit.

Customer-managed keys (BYOK)

Encryption keys held in your KMS. Innovent never sees customer key material.

Full data sovereignty

Data resides in your account, your region. No cross-tenant exposure surface.

Infrastructure-level audit

Customer security teams have CloudTrail / Azure Monitor / GCP audit-log access to the deployment.

Compliance alignment

NIST 800-53, HIPAA, PCI-DSS-aligned baseline. Customer-specific controls overlay-able.

Managed updates

Innovent maintains the platform. Customer controls when updates apply.

Cloud economics with multi-tenancy off the table. Your VPC, your keys, your audit access. Innovent operates the platform inside your boundary.
