The Innfini Architecture Reference
White paper · PDF in preparation

This is the canonical technical reference for the Innfini platform. Architects, solution engineers, and customer technical teams use it as the source of truth for deployment planning, capacity estimation, and integration design.

The paper is structured around the five-layer architecture: sensor fabric, connectors, object graph, decision engine, and agentic orchestration. Each layer is covered with its responsibilities, interfaces, data flows, deployment patterns, and operational characteristics.

What's inside

Section 1 covers the platform philosophy — why a layered, typed, agentic architecture instead of a data-warehouse-plus-model-gateway. Section 2 through 6 cover each layer in depth.

Sections 7 and 8 cover deployment topologies — managed cloud, private cloud, on-prem, hybrid edge, and air-gapped. Each topology comes with capacity planning guidance and reference diagrams.

Section 9 covers operational characteristics — SLAs, throughput numbers, failure modes, recovery patterns. Section 10 is the appendix: schemas, API references, security controls.
