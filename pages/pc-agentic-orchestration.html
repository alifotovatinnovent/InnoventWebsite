// How agentic orchestration works

Six stages. One continuous loop.

Every agent flows through the same reasoning loop — and every step is recorded with provenance, citations and operator-controllable approvals.

  1. 01 · Observe

    Watch every signal

    Monitor events from RFID, IoT, vision, workflows and enterprise systems in real time.

  2. 02 · Understand

    Build context

    Pull asset history, user role, location, SLA, severity and business context from the object graph.

  3. 03 · Plan

    Author the playbook

    Compose an action plan grounded in SOPs, rules and operational priorities — typed and verifiable.

  4. 04 · Decide

    Under guardrails

    Apply RBAC, approval thresholds and policy — auto, attended, or supervised by configuration.

  5. 05 · Act

    Trigger workflow

    Create work orders, send alerts, escalate, update ERP/WMS — through the integration layer.

  6. 06 · Explain

    Cited reasoning

    Every recommendation and action ships with reasoning + citations to the underlying records.
