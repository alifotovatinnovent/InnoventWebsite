// Why it matters

The problem

  • 01
    LLM hallucinations. Unattached LLMs fabricate operational facts. In production environments, this is unacceptable.
  • 02
    Multilingual ops. Major operations span dozens of language communities. English-only conversational AI excludes most operators.
  • 03
    Voice-first environments. Ops floors are loud, hands-full, gloved-handed environments. Text-only interfaces don't cut it.
  • 04
    Answers without action. A conversational AI that can only answer questions is half a product. The other half is taking action.
