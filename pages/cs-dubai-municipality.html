// Challenges

The brief

  • 01
    Diverse smart-city domains. City-wide Municipality operations span fragmented monitoring and siloed data across municipal facilities and assets.
  • 02
    Manual fault detection. Slow incident response and high compliance reporting effort across irrigation, waste, security and environmental systems.
  • 03
    No unified view. Lack of a single operational view across multiple sensor types and use cases — delaying anomaly detection and affecting service continuity.
  • 04
    Single smart-city fabric needed. A platform unifying every IoT domain and operational use case under one operating picture.
// Solution

Innfini as the operating layer.

Innovent deployed Innfini as the smart-city operations platform for Dubai Municipality — powering city-wide smart-city use cases under one unified IoT and operations fabric.

Smart irrigation

Automated irrigation control and water-flow telemetry across municipal parks and green spaces.

Crowd management & CCTV

Real-time crowd monitoring, situational awareness and integrated video intelligence.

Access control

Unified physical-security telemetry across municipal facilities and sites.

Waste management

Sensor-driven bin monitoring, route optimization and waste-collection orchestration.

Environmental quality

Compliance monitoring and anomaly alerts across diverse municipal services.

Sensor-agnostic gateway

GIS- and map-based reporting, configurable dashboards and CEP rules — extensible to new use cases.

One smart-city operating picture for Dubai Municipality — irrigation, waste, CCTV, environment, access control. Every sensor, every domain, every use case.
