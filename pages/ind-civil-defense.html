02 / Platform

Seven surfaces. One platform.

INNFINI combines RFID asset monitoring, mobile handhelds, officer wearables, vehicle telematics, AI-assisted dashboards and incident-zone intelligence into one operational command layer.

A · Trucks

RFID truck readiness

Fixed RFID readers inside fire trucks auto-detect tagged equipment. Real-time presence checks. Missing items flagged before departure.

B · Mobile

Handheld field ops

Rugged RFID/barcode handhelds for cycle counts, Geiger-style lookup, transfers, custodian changes — Wi-Fi or batch sync.

C · Officers

Vital tracking

BLE officer ID + biometric wearable streams heart rate, SpO2, temperature, fall detection and indoor/outdoor location to command.

D · Command

Incident command

Live map with vehicle markers, draw-zone tally of vehicles, personnel, water, foam and equipment — formatted for the unit leader.

E · Vehicles

Telematics & resources

Vehicle telematics, shutter sensors, water and foam levels, fuel, engine diagnostics — connected into truck-resource cards.

F · AI

Next-best action

AI proposes vehicle assignment, safety escalations and pre-departure gating. Cited reasoning. Operator approves.

Challenges
  • →Nation-wide Civil Defense operations across 100+ stations — fragmented systems with limited integration across asset, fleet, health, video and emergency response.
  • →Lack of real-time visibility into vehicle status, asset availability, officer location, vital signs and equipment readiness across the national fleet.
  • →Manual, siloed processes degrading response time, situational awareness and field-to-command coordination at national scale.
  • →Need for a single national command-and-control fabric unifying every mission-critical system into one operating picture.
Solution

Deployed INNFini as the national-level Command-and-Control system for Abu Dhabi Civil Defense — rolled out across 100+ stations and unifying 13+ existing mission-critical systems across personnel, vehicles, stations and assets.

  • •Fleet telemetry, vehicle diagnostics & predictive-maintenance feeds for real-time fleet performance monitoring.
  • •Continuous officer monitoring — vital-signs wearables and live GPS location tracking for every officer in the field.
  • •Water-flow / water-level and foam-level sensors for continuous monitoring of firefighting resources.
  • •Bay-door & shutter sensors monitoring every station entry and exit in real time.
  • •Unified video intelligence — in-vehicle cameras, dash cams, body-worn cams & station CCTV for full situational awareness.
  • •Reporting & Analytics, asset & equipment management and emergency dispatch — all surfaced through a single operating picture.
