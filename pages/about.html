What is Innovent?

Innovent Tech Solutions (innovent.io) designs and delivers Innfini, a low-code / no-code operational intelligence platform for mission-critical and enterprise operations. We help organizations unify operational data and workflows so teams can see, decide, and act with accountability.

We are not “just an RFID company.” Sensing technologies matter as inputs; the category we own is Enterprise Operational Intelligence & Digital Transformation via Innfini.

What is Innfini?

Innfini by Innovent Tech Solutions is the AI-native operating layer for enterprise operational intelligence. It fuses sensor feeds, video, and enterprise systems into a live operating picture, with agents and automation that run under your policy and leave an audit trail.
