// Challenges

The brief

  • 01
    Heavy paper-based workflows. Kingdom-wide MoI operations across ministries, facilities and field services relied on paper, manual approvals and disconnected legacy systems.
  • 02
    Fragmented documentation. HR access control, evidence handling and critical-asset management were siloed — limiting operational efficiency and traceability.
  • 03
    Compliance & visibility risk. Delayed decision-making and increased compliance risk across critical facilities; no real-time visibility into approvals, assets or environmental conditions.
  • 04
    Enterprise-scale transformation needed. Eliminating paper, standardizing processes and unifying every operation under one platform — the explicit MoI mandate.
// Solution

Innfini as the operating layer.

Innovent deployed Innfini as the enterprise digital-transformation platform for the Saudi Ministry of Interior — converting MoI to fully paperless operations and unifying its critical workflows under one platform.

Paperless approvals

Every form, request and approval digitized through configurable workflows and rules.

HR access control

Identity governance fully integrated with physical-security and badge systems across MoI facilities.

Evidence track-and-trace

Full chain-of-custody, immutable audit trails and role-based access across the enterprise.

Critical-asset management

Weapons, equipment and sensitive items tracked end-to-end with lifecycle workflows and real-time status.

IoT telemetry

Temperature, humidity and environment sensors ingested via MQTT for proactive facility monitoring.

Rules & alerts

Configurable thresholds trigger proactive notifications on breaches — ensuring compliance and operational standards.

A kingdom-wide ministry, fully paperless — every approval, every asset, every evidence chain, every environmental reading on one platform.
// Business impact

The outcome.

  • 01
    Paper eliminated. Complete removal of paper-based processes across MoI through end-to-end digital workflows.
  • 02
    Unified enterprise platform. Multiple legacy and standalone systems integrated into one operational view across the Ministry.
  • 03
    Real-time monitoring. Environmental conditions, evidence and critical assets visible in real time across MoI facilities.
  • 04
    Compliance & audit-ready. Automated alerts and tamper-evident audit trails across the entire enterprise.
