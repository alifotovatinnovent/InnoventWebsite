01

RFID inventory & supply-chain automation

Inventory counted by hand is slow, periodic and error-prone. By the time a stock count is finished the data is already out of date — stalling shipments and inflating cost. Every item instead carries an RFID tag and is read automatically as it moves: no line of sight, no manual scanning. Data entry becomes a by-product of normal operations.

Reads
Patented long-range RFID — hundreds of items at once, no line of sight, passive-tag economics
Location
True RTLS using statistical radiometric methods across multiple receivers, not simple antenna association
Dock doors
Direction of travel and moving-vs-stationary discrimination; ML reduces shielding between adjacent doors
Integration
Automated capture straight into ERP / SAP
02

ForkEye — machine-vision pallet tracking

Turn every forklift into a moving data-capture point. Pallets are scanned by machine vision the moment a forklift picks or drops them, in the plant or the yard, and the forklift's GPS locks a precise coordinate to each one. The map then shows every item's last scanned location, its timestamp and the forklift that moved it — with no manual data entry at all.

Hardware
IP66 720p anti-vibration camera plus an on-board edge processing unit, Wi-Fi or 4G
Recognition
AI image processing and object recognition on standard 2D barcodes, trainable for basic checks
Infrastructure
Minimum fixed infrastructure — low cost, quick to deploy, easy to expand
Reporting
Live inventory map, movement playback, filters by date, material, batch, zone, grade and type

03

IoT environmental monitoring

In manufacturing, ambient conditions decide product quality. Temperature drift, humidity spikes, poor air quality or pressure changes can spoil a batch and breach compliance — and they often go unrecorded until an auditor or a customer complaint surfaces the problem. A mesh of low-cost wireless sensors measures conditions continuously and feeds INNFINI, turning guesswork into a defensible record of the conditions every product was made in.

Measures
Temperature, humidity, air quality and CO₂, pressure, and the rest of the process spectrum
Alerts
The CEP rules engine fires the instant a reading breaches a limit — email, HTTP, pub/sub or web socket
Evidence
Every reading logged for audit, with historical playback of what happened and when
04

Condition monitoring & predictive maintenance

Critical and semi-critical motors are typically the only assets with condition monitoring — yet they are just 15–25% of the fleet. The rest of the balance-of-plant equipment runs to failure, because traditional monitoring has been too expensive or too ineffective to deploy at scale. Low-cost wireless beacons measuring vibration and temperature, with edge processing and cloud AI, extend predictive maintenance to the whole fleet.

Sense
A wireless beacon on each asset measures vibration and temperature with intelligent edge processing
Learn
AI establishes each machine's own baseline, so deviation is judged against that asset, not a generic threshold
Act
Immediate notification when equipment stops or exceeds temperature thresholds
05

Command & control centre

Large organisations run dozens of disconnected systems — IoT, security, building management, ERP, GIS — each with its own screen and its own alarms. In an incident, operators swivel between consoles and correlate by hand. The INNFINI control tower ingests from every source, correlates events in real time and presents one operational picture, with response playbooks automating the next step.

Layer 1
Data acquisition — technology-agnostic ingestion of RFID, sensors, machine vision, GPS, Bluetooth and third-party systems
Layer 2
Correlation and rules — the CEP engine and the visual INNFINI Flow composer
Layer 3
Operational picture — dashboards, map reports, playbooks and role-based sharing
