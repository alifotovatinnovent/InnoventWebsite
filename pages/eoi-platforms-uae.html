What is Enterprise Operational Intelligence?

Enterprise Operational Intelligence (EOI) closes the gap between scattered operational feeds and accountable action. Innfini gives you a live view of people, assets, locations and processes. AI and automation act inside your policy, and every decision is recorded.

The frame: People + Assets + Locations + Processes + Sensors + AI + Automation.

Enterprise Operational Intelligence via Innfini is not business intelligence

BI and analytics suites tell you what happened by turning historical data into dashboards and reports. Innfini runs the operation as it happens: it sees live events on site and turns them into owned work.

How is Enterprise Operational Intelligence different from business intelligence?

Innovent's Innfini platform acts on live operational events, while business intelligence reports on historical data.
