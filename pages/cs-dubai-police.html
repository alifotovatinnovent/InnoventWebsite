// Challenges

The brief

  • 01
    Force-wide silos. Operations span multiple services and locations with 20+ internal Dubai-Police-built systems operating in silos.
  • 02
    Manual reconciliation. Weapons, critical assets and high-value items logged manually — limited audit traceability across armory and checkpoints.
  • 03
    Compliance risk. Risk of unauthorized access or movement of critical assets; fragmented compliance across existing operational systems.
  • 04
    Smart-station rollout. A central digital-transformation platform was needed to unify every internal system into one operating fabric — including the new intelligent smart police stations.
